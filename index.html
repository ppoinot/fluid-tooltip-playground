One pill for every icon

The tooltip never closes between icons. The same element glides to the next one on a spring.

Width follows the label

The pill springs to the new label's width, so "Today" stretches into a full name without a jump.

Text rolls the way you move

The old label leaves and the new one arrives from the direction of travel, blurred while it moves.

It waits only once

The first hover waits for the open delay. Along the rail, or coming back within the skip window, it shows at once.
